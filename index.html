Approach

Three ideas we borrow from the sky.

Navigators measure progress against fixed points that do not move with the weather. We hold capital to the same standard.

Parallax

two vantage points, one true position

A star's distance is found by viewing it from two places. We read every opportunity twice: once for its financial merit and once for its effect on people and planet. Only where both readings agree do we see it clearly.

Long exposure

patience reveals what a snapshot misses

Faint light only appears when the shutter stays open. Systems change slowly, and so does durable value. We favor structures and time frames that let real outcomes surface.

True north

a fixed reference in shifting conditions

Markets drift and frameworks come and go. Every commitment begins with the purpose the capital must serve, and that purpose becomes the bearing for every later decision.
